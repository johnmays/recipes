# Butter Caper Salmon & Spinach

## Ingredients
- salmon
- spinach
- parsley
- lemons
- capers
- pepper
- butter unsalted
- shallots
- 2 cloves garlic
- dry white wine
- crusty baguette

## Process
- salmon, skin on
- pat dry both sides.  plenty of salt & pepper both sides
- mise en place:
  - shallots tiny
  - parsley tiny
  - garlic thin slices
- cook salmon:
  - get pan hot
  - add plenty of oil
  - add salmon skin down
  - baste in oil
  - cook salmon completely
  - let rest
  - get oil out
- sauce:
  - brown a couple of pieces of butter
  - add capers to hot butter
  - add lemon juice -- a few tbsp
  - add white wine splash
  - sauce out
- cook spinach:
  - add a few tbsp of butter
  - cook shallots & onion
  - add spinach
  - a little white wine
  - dollop of dijon
  - finish with lemon juice & cracked pepper
 
- when serving:
  - plate salmon
  - plate spinach
  - add parsley to sauce
  - add sauce
  - add bread

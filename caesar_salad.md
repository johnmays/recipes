# Vegetarian Caesar Salad
No chicken, no anchovies, although you could fry up tofu like chicken if you wanted.

[Video](https://youtu.be/Y9w89EU8o-E?si=tHXv_cdpYz8sEY_H)

## Ingredients:
### salad:
- olive oil
- 1 egg
- 1 head of romaine lettuce
- 1/4 to 1/2 of a stale or fresh baguette or crusty bread
- lemon
- garlic
- dijon mustard
- parmesan block
- black pepper
### veg Worcestershire sauce:
This is how I would attempt to imitate Worcestershire, but quite honestly soy sauce on its own would probably work okay
- soy sauce
- ketchup
- a non-white vinegar (apple cider, red wine, rice, etc.)

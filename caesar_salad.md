# Vegetarian Caesar Salad
Hand-made croutons and dressing are the main features.  No chicken, no anchovies, although you could fry up tofu like chicken if you wanted.
## Sources:
- [Video](https://youtu.be/Y9w89EU8o-E?si=tHXv_cdpYz8sEY_H)
- [Gordon Ramsay](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IGlWE4AFQ5Q&ab_channel=Dabl)
## Ingredients:
The following list of ingredients is for a single serving, a personal salad.  If you choose to scale up, not every ingredient, namely the amount of croutons & the egg/oil mixture, need to be scaled proportionally.  The recipe is somewhat flexible
### salad:
- 1 egg yolk
- ~tbsp olive oil (goal is equivalent volume to egg yolk)
- 4 big or 5 medium leaves of romaine lettuce
- a healthy handful of a stale or fresh baguette or crusty bread
- 1/4 lemon's lemon juice
- 1-1.5 medium-sized cloves garlic (sometimes I do 2, but I like garlic & 2 is quite a bit)
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/4 cup parmesan
- black pepper
- 3.5 tsp veg custom Worcestershire sauce
### veg Worcestershire sauce:
This is how I would attempt to imitate Worcestershire, but quite honestly soy sauce on its own would probably work okay.  You can also find vegan replacements at the grocery.  Not so sure those are healthy.
### for a single serving
- 1.5 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp ketchup
- 1 tsp a non-white vinegar (apple cider, red wine, rice, etc.) (red wine vinegar is best)

## Instructions:
### Prep:
#### Start with croutons and while they're cooking, do the rest
- cut your volume of bread into crouton-size cubes
- use a few tsp of olive oil to coat the bread cubes
- put them in a pan, coat with a pinch of salt and 3-5 cranks of pepper (very easy to overdo the pepper in this recipe)
- heat on quite a low setting until the faces brown
- turn the heat off and grate a bit of parmesan on to the croutons
- shuffle the croutons to coat evenly
#### the rest:
In a big bowl, create the dressing:

- add olive oil
- crack egg, use your hand to select the yolk, and place in bowl
- crush garlic or dice pretty fine -> into bowl
- whisk the snot out of it like you are trying to create mayonnaise
- add the dijon, a few cranks of black pepper, the "worcestershire"
- squeeze the lemon in
- mix
- grate the parmesan in
- mix

Put everything together:
- chop the lettuce into inch-wide strips (cut perpendicular to line of symmetry)
- add lettuce to bowl, mix
- add 2/3 of croutons, mix
- serve with rest of croutons on top
- grate a bit more parmesan on as a topping

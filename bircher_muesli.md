# Bircher Muesli (My Recipe)
Inspired by Ben Lerner's _Transcription_ and healthy German people.

You make a dry mix, and the night before you want to eat it for breakfast, you add the wet ingredients.
## Ingredients:
### Dry:
- Rolled (Old-Fashioned) Oats
- Golden Raisins
- Walnuts
- Pumpkin seeds

### Wet:
- Milk
- Honey
- Lemon Juice
- Apple

## Instructions:
### Dry:
- in big jar:
  - 6 parts oats
  - 1 part pumpkin seeds
  - 1 part walnuts
  - 1 part raisins

### Wet:
The night before you eat it for breakfast:
- In a 16oz (2 cup) wide-mouth mason jar, add:
  - 1.5 cup dry muesli mix
  - 1 tbsp honey
  - half a lemon of juice
  - half or third of grated apple
  - enough milk to cover it all

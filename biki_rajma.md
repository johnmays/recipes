# Rajma (Indian red bean curry)
A version of the Punjab-style rajma cooked & eaten by bodybuilder Biki Singh (<3). Serve with long white rice.

## Sources:
- [Biki Video](https://youtu.be/7COLGrmN9ZQ?si=xgmu1ffR73s2VBvF)
## Ingredients:
- white onion
- tomato
- garlic, ginger
- red kidney beans
- tsp of turmeric
- tsp of coriander
- tsp of red chili powder
- garam masala
- tsp of ghee (butter is fine substitute)

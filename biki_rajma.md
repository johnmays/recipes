# Rajma (Indian red bean curry)
A version of the Punjab-style rajma cooked & eaten by bodybuilder Biki Singh (<3). Serve with long white rice.

## Sources:
- [Biki Video](https://youtu.be/7COLGrmN9ZQ?si=7mjOvEuznc0rnNP2&t=138)
## Ingredients:
(These portions make 5 servings)
- half of a large white onion
- 2 small or 1 medium tomato 
- 3 tbsp garlic ginger paste; add more to taste if necessary
- 2 16oz cans of red kidney beans
- 3 tsp of turmeric
- 3 tsp of coriander
- 3 tsp of red chili powder
- 3 tsp garam masala
- 4 tsp of salt
- 5 tsp of ghee (butter is fine substitute)

# Rajma (Indian red bean curry)
A version of the Punjab-style rajma cooked & eaten by bodybuilder Biki Singh (<3). Serve with long white rice.

## Sources:
- [Biki Video](https://youtu.be/7COLGrmN9ZQ?si=7mjOvEuznc0rnNP2&t=138)
## Ingredients:
(These portions make 8 servings)
- half of a large white onion
- 1 small tomato
- 3 tsp garlic ginger paste; add more to taste if necessary
- 2 16oz cans of red kidney beans
- 1 tsp of turmeric
- 1.5 tsp of coriander
- 1 tsp of red chili powder (1.5 for mom)
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- 1-2 tsp of salt (really, do it to taste because garam masala & the beans will have plenty of it)
- 2 tsp of ghee (butter is fine substitute)

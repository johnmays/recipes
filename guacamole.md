# My Guacamole

## Ingredients
- 5-6 large Haas avocadoes, squishy
- half a red tomato
- half a red onion
- half a bunch of cilantro
- 1 jalapeno or serrano
- 2-3 cloves of garlic
- (don't forget to get chips)
- juice of 1-1.5 limes
- a bunch of salt

## Instructions:
- Dice avocadoes, tomato, cilantro, pepper, and onion
- Mince garlic
- Squish lime
- Mix

# Korean Braised Tofu
- This is simple.  It takes like 20 minutes, not counting rice.
- [Source Video](https://youtube.com/shorts/M6n31FgoJnc?si=OBpcCIrb5_mQBhiE)

## Ingredients:
- 1 block of firm non-silken tofu, maybe even medium if you're feeling kinda slutty
- White or brown short-grain rice
- Soy sauce
- Gochugaru (flakes)
- Honey
- Rice vinegar
- Garlic
- Green onions
- Sesame erl
- Sesame seeds

## Steps:
### 0. Since, you eat this with rice, cook white or brown rice.

### 1. Prepare tofu:
- Get a pack of firm non-silken tofu
- Slice into eight pieces maybe half a centimeter thickness.
- pad dry, sprinkle a little salt on either side, let rest 10 minutes

### 2. Prepare braising liquid while tofu sits:
Mix:
  - 1 tbsp honey
  - 1.5 tbsp korean chili flakes
  - 1/3 cup water
  - 1/3 cup soy sauce
  - 1.5 tsp rice vinegar
  - 4 cloves garlic sliced into thin Goodfellas-like slivers
  - lower halves of 2 green onions
  - 1 tbsp sesame erl
  - 1 tbsp sesame seeds

### 3. Braise:
- a coat of neutral erl in pan
- cook tofu in oil 2-3 minutes each side - until browning is witnessed
- add braising liquid
- 3-5 more minutes until liquid reduces

### 4. Garnish with the tops of the green onions.

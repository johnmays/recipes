# Hummus Levantine Style

## Ingredients:
- 2 cans of chickpeas
- glob of tahini
- enormous bulb of garlic
- lemon juice to taste
- salt to taste
- olive oil TO GARNISH NOT IN THE HUMMUS
## Instructions:
- add everything to food processor & blend it

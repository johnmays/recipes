# Whole Bowl

## First, you gotta make a batch of Tali Sauce:

Blend:
- 1/2 cup olive erl
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup nutritional yeast
- 2 eggs
- 2 lemons of juice
- a couple cloves of garlic
- big tablespoon of mustard
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 tsp chili powder

## Then make the bowl:
- brown rice
- black
  - & kidney beans (1:1)
- sauce
- mild cheddar shredded
- red salsa
- cilantro
- sour cream (optional)
- half an avocado
- Tabasco green jalapeno (optional)
- Orange hot sauce (optional)

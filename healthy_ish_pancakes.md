# Healthy-Ish Pancakes
Contains oats & whole-wheat flour.  Makes six big pancakes.
## Ingredients:
- 1.25 cups milk
- 1.25 cups rolled oats
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 0.5 cup white flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1.5 tsp baking powder

## Prepare Mix:
- let oats & milk sit in bowl 5 min
- add eggs & oil
- beat
- stir in flour, sugar, baking powder, salt

## Cook
- oil on cast iron
- make sure batter is thick enough
- pour. with oil
- wait for bubbles on top
- cook other side 2 or 3 minutes

## Serve:
- berries, maple syrup, butter

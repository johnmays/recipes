# Vegetarian Reuben
Classic Jewish reuben using tofu.  Makes a large batch of "meat" than you can then repeatedly reheat for 5-7 sandwiches.
## Sources:
- [Vegan corned "beef" (tofu)](https://thehiddenveggies.com/vegan-corned-beef/)

## Ingredients:
- pumpernickel loaf... you could also do Jewish rye
- 2 12-14oz packages of extra firm tofu
- Thousand island dressing
- saurkraut
- butter
- block of swiss cheese
- spices
    - 1 tbsp of pickling spic
    - 1 tsp mustard seeds
    - 1 tsp coriander seeds
    - 1.5 tbsp salt
    - 1 leaf bay leaf
    - 1 tsp brown sugar

## Instructions:
### Make the tofu:
- start 6 cups of water boiling with the salt
- once it is boiling, add the rest of the spices
- cut the tofu into a little less that 1/3" slices.  Should be like 10-14 slices per block.
- after the spices have been boiling for 10 minutes, boil the tofu for 10-12 minutes.
- take off the heat after the tofu has boiled, but let the pieces sit in the hot brine for 30 minutes.
- preheat oven for 375°F
- bake for about an hour as well as you can spread the pieces out on a baking sheet.  Flip them once after 30 minutes.
### Prep the sandwich:
- spread 1000 island on two pieces of bread.  
- ;ay swiss, "meat", and saurkraut in between, then close the sandwich.
- butter both sides of the sandwich.
- grill in a pan for about 5 minutes each side (medium heat); you should really be cooking until the bread is crispy.  
    - it's best to to do the cheese side closer to the pan last so you deal with it melting for less time.

# Leek & Lentil Pot Pie
#FrenchMomDinner 
## Sources:
[Ripped almost directly from Capucine Emily on YouTube](https://youtu.be/R0oV4WSf-2I?si=iPmybdVff7-zt-ze)
## Ingredients:
- 2 leeks
- 2 cans pre-cooked lentils
- 1 sheet puff pastry
- olive oil
- 1-1.5 cup heavy cream
- 1 zucchini
- 2 lemons
- better than bouillion 1 tbsp (veg flavor)
- black peppa
- garlic 3 cloves

## Instructions:
### Prep:
- set puff pastry out of freezer to thaw
- split & wash the fucking sand out of your leeks
- chop up leeks into pot pie-sized pieces
- same w/ zucchini
- mince garlic
- get zest of two lemons
- preheat oven to 425F
### Main Process:
in a cast iron pan,
- add leeks w/ olive oil
- cook for a few min
- add zucchini & garlic
- add salt
- cook until leek edges brown
- add cream & tbsp of bouillion & lentil
- cook for 10 min
- turn off heat
- squeeze in 1.5 lemons juice
- add black pepper
- taste for salt & acid
- lay puff pastry over top of cast iron.  add slits, drizzle olive oil
- let bake for 20 minutes.

# Batata Harra - Lebanese Spicy Potatoes
[source](https://youtu.be/2ST23bd-Lao?si=meSsMpN0yhgjrUhu)

## Ingredients
- enough golden and red potatoes to fill a baking tray
- parsley
- 2 lemons
- yogurt
- tahini
- garlic
- at least 2 dry chili forms (I like Korean pepper flakes plus Korean pepper powder, plus regular crushed red pepper)
- arugala (not necessary, but I like to eat it w/ this)
- corn starch
- tomato paste
- olive oil

## Instructions
### 1. potatoes
- cut up potatoes
- set on baking tray
- toss in olive oil, corn starch, salt, pepper
- bake for 50 min at 400. toss once
### 2. yogurt bedding
- mix 1 cup of yogurt
- 4 tbsp tahini
- juice of 1 lemon
- 2 garlic cloves minced
### 3. final prep
- mince 3 cloves of garlic
- add glug of olive erl to pan
- add tbsp of tomato paste, garlic, 3 tsp of your dried chili forms in whatever proportion you like
- add a little salt
- turn on heat
- wait a few minutes for it to become red oil
- add parsley and squeeze in half a lemon
- turn off heat
- add potatoes
- toss
- serve potatoes and arugala on top of yogurt bedding

# Soft-Boiled Eggs

## Reminders
- eggs are sensitive to motion
    - salting water makes eggs slightly more bouyant and prone to oscillatory bouncing. So don't salt water.
- getting yolk right is sensitive to time and size of egg
- Should work to reasonable degree for any size of egg
- probably better to go under than over 7 min

## Instructions

- boil water (NO SALT) above egg height
- put eggs into water with spoon
- immediately begin 7 minute timer
- wait a minute until boil resumes
    - then, try to make it a light boil
- prepare bowl of ice water
- take eggs out right at 7 minutes, put into ice water

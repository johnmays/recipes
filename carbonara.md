# Meatless Carbonara
## Sources:
- [Recipe website](https://www.notanothercookingshow.tv/post/Cacio-E-Uova)
- [Video](https://youtu.be/yJKFNr6xwYg)

## Ingredients:
- 1 heaping bowl of dry pasta. all of these work:
    - tubetti
    - farfalle
    - rigatoni
- small block of pecorino romano ($\geq$ 1 cup loose fine shredded)
- small block of parmigiano reggiano ($\geq$ 1 cup loose fine shredded)
- 3 small eggs or 2 large eggs
small handful of fresh parsley
- breadcrumbs
- quarter stick of butter
- a few cloves of garlic
- salt & pepper
## Instructions:
### Preparation:
- grate equivalent portions of the two cheeses
- start water boiling w/ salt
- chop parsley
### Going at it:
#### _Note:_ Pasta, sauce, and breadcrumbs should all be done simultaneously
- put pasta in for around 9 minutes and melt the butter on low heat with a smushed clove of garlic in the pan
- brown the butter
- once fairly brown but not dark, take all of butter and set aside in little recepticle
- put breadcrumbs into pan immediately
- put half of parsley into pan as well
- cook breadcrumbs until a noticeably darker and dry to the taste
- remove breadcrumbs from heat
- whisk (remaining parsley, both cheeses, all eggs) together in heat-resistant bowl on top of pasta (45 seconds on, 45 seconds off) (whisk more while on)
- after a few cycles, slowly whisk in remaining brown butter to sauce
- wait for sauce to thicken
    - you may have to finish sauce after pasta is done
    - you may have to add a little corn starch to pasta to thicken up sauce (or more cheese) (corn starch is easy, but may make reheated leftovers worse)
- mix sauce and pasta, add breadcrumbs to top, serve

# Shrimp Ceviche (Mexican?)
Makes 6 or so servings
## Ingredients:
- 1.5 lbs shrimp
- 5 or 6 limes
- 4 roma tomatoes
- 1-1.5 bunches of green onions
- 1 small red onion
- 1 medium-sized slicing cucumber
- 1 bunch cilantro
- red hot sauce (Tapatio might be too vinegar-y.  Pico Pica or Valentina would be best I think)
- 1 serrano
- tortilla chips thick and made in avocado oil

## Instructions
- set salted water to boil
- split cucumber and tomatoes. excise goop
- chop tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, serrano, & cilantro into tiny pieces
- chop only white part of green onions
- peel shrimp
- add to water & par boil
- squeeze limes while shrimp cooks
- chop boiled shrimp into small pieces
- add shrimp pieces to lime juice
- let stand 15 minutes
- add salt, lemon juice, and hot sauce (conservatively) to ceviche to taste
